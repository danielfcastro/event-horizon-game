## 2. Hard design rule (PLAN.md section 8, verbatim)

> Do not make the game a pure power fantasy.
>
> More mass should create tension:
>
> ```text
> more mass = stronger pull
> more mass = larger collision risk
> more mass = slower precise movement
> more mass = harder navigation
> more mass = stability may decay
> ```
>
> This gives players real decisions:
>
> ```text
> Grow fast and risk instability?
> Stay smaller and move precisely?
> Upgrade for efficiency or pull?
> Absorb large objects or avoid hazards?
> ```

Every content item you define must serve that dial. Nothing you add may switch it
off. A hazard that is pure punishment with no decision is a rule violation.

## 3. PLAN.md excerpts this artifact depends on

### PLAN 5.4 Black hole stats

Mass (main progression), Event Horizon Radius (visual gravitational boundary),
Pull Strength, Absorption Radius, Speed, Stability (prevents runaway growth or
overload), Efficiency (how much absorbed mass becomes useful mass), Combo
(streak bonus for chaining absorptions).

### PLAN 5.5 Starting black hole types

**Star Mass Black Hole** — theme: stable, reliable, slow-growth. Traits: stable
mass growth, reliable pull, better absorption efficiency, lower volatility, good
for beginners. Stats: Mass stable / Pull medium / Efficiency high / Stability
high / Speed medium.

**Quantum Black Hole** — theme: unstable, high-power, high-risk. Traits:
stronger pull, faster growth, more volatile, can emit quantum particles, can
phase-shift absorption modes, can chain-react with anomalies, higher risk of
instability. Stats: Mass volatile / Pull high / Efficiency medium / Stability
low / Speed high.

(A-007 set the numeric stat blocks: Star pullScale 1.8 / eff 0.90 / stab 110 /
speed 24 / absorbFactor 0.34; Quantum pullScale 2.5 / eff 0.75 / stab 70 /
speed 29 / absorbFactor 0.26, massGain rolls ×[0.85,1.25] seeded, stability
drains ×1.5. Names and lore are yours; the numbers are not.)

### PLAN 5.7 Object types

```text
Small:   Dust, Pebbles, Ice fragments, Small debris
Medium:  Rocks, Metal shards, Ship fragments, Asteroids
Large:   Asteroid chunks, Small planets, Star fragments, Quantum anomalies
Hazards: Mines, Gravity anchors, Magnetic fields, Collapsing debris, Massive objects
```

### PLAN 5.8 Level goals (examples)

Reach target mass. Survive time. Maintain combo. Avoid hazards. Absorb
anomalies. Use efficiency challenge.

### PLAN 5.9 Upgrade systems

Two systems: permanent meta-progression and temporary run upgrades.

Permanent: Gravity Lens, Accretion Core, Horizon Stabilizer, Inertial Dampener,
Combo Anchor, Quantum Core, Star Core.

Temporary run upgrades (PLAN reference values, kept by A-007): +15% pull
strength, +10% absorption radius, +10% movement speed, +10% mass efficiency,
+1 quantum anomaly spawn.

### PLAN 5.10 Progression curve

Diminishing returns: mass gain direct, horizon grows with sqrt(mass), pull with
mass^0.75, speed decreases slightly as mass grows. Target anchors: L1 1,000 ·
L5 10,000 · L10 50,000 · L20 250,000 · L40 1,000,000.

### PLAN 5.11 Failure conditions

Time limit; stability reaches zero; too many hazard collisions; mass drops below
threshold. Recommended: time limit + stability + hazard pressure.

### PLAN 5.12 Campaign worlds

- **World 1 Dust Belt** — teaches movement, attraction, absorption. Dust,
  pebbles, small rocks. No hazards or very few.
- **World 2 Asteroid Field** — teaches larger objects and clustering. Rocks,
  asteroids, metal shards.
- **World 3 Ship Graveyard** — teaches high-value targets and path planning.
  Ship fragments, mines, magnetic fields.
- **World 4 Planet Ring** — teaches large mass and stability management. Planet
  fragments, gravity anchors, heavy objects.
- **World 5 Quantum Nebula** — teaches quantum mode, phase shifts, chain
  reactions. Quantum anomalies, phase particles, unstable cores.

### PLAN 5.13 Endgame modes

Campaign, endless mode, daily challenge, weekly challenge, leaderboards.

### PLAN 5.14 Monetization

Free-to-play, no pay-to-win, optional ads, optional IAP for cosmetics, optional
ad removal, optional premium version. Ads only at game over / retry / between
levels / optional rewarded. Never during gameplay, no forced interstitials, no
aggressive ad walls.

## 4. Upstream decisions you must honor (do not renumber)

### From A-007 docs/balance.md (done)

- Body mass tiers, scaled by level tier multiplier T(n) = target(n)/1000:
  Dust 3–10, Pebble 15–40, Chunk 60–150, Anomaly 300–800.
- Stability pools: 100 baseline / 110 Star / 70 Quantum. Hazard drains by size
  tier: small 8, medium 14, large 22. Oversized-absorb drains by body/player
  mass ratio: ≤0.5× → 0, 0.5–1.0× → 6, 1.0–2.0× → 14, >2.0× → 26. Quantum
  multiplies all drains ×1.5. Regen +2/s after 6 s clean.
- Hazard budgets per world (live hazards): Dust Belt 6, Asteroid Field 10,
  Ship Graveyard 14, Planet Ring 18, Quantum Nebula 22.
- Permanent upgrade effects/costs are A-007's (Gravity Lens +8% pull/rank,
  Accretion Core +4% efficiency, Horizon Stabilizer +10 stability pool,
  Inertial Dampener +6% speed, Combo Anchor +0.5 s window, Star/Quantum Core
  unlock types; 2,195 cores total). Run upgrade values are A-007's.
- Economy: Stellar Cores, earned by play only; level clear 25+5n, first clear
  ×2, secondary goal +40%, 3-star +50%.
- Efficiency capped at 1.00; no upgrade reduces drain tables.
- Mass ceiling 2^24; timers 150+5n seconds; feedback bands at 0.30/0.75 of
  target.

Your content must map onto these numbers, not redefine them.

### From A-006 docs/design.md (done)

- Shape-first visual language (design §3.2): absorbable bodies are rounded/
  organic silhouettes; hazards are sharp/spiky silhouettes plus a marker glyph
  (bracket/X family), never color-only. Your hazard definitions must state the
  silhouette and glyph family for each hazard so colorblind players can read it.
- Contrast hierarchy: event horizon ring > hazard markers > large bodies >
  medium bodies > small bodies > field texture.
- Off-screen indicators (design §3.4): only bodies currently being pulled, or
  hazards whose approach vector intersects the horizon; max 8; chevron vs
  bracketed-X by shape.
- Ring-fracture is the stability anchor cue; content must not fight it.
