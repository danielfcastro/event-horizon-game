## Hard design rule (PLAN.md section 8 — verbatim)

> Do not make the game a pure power fantasy.
>
> More mass should create tension:
>
> ```text
> more mass = stronger pull
> more mass = larger collision risk
> more mass = slower precise movement
> more mass = harder navigation
> more mass = stability may decay
> ```
>
> This gives players real decisions:
>
> ```text
> Grow fast and risk instability?
> Stay smaller and move precisely?
> Upgrade for efficiency or pull?
> Absorb large objects or avoid hazards?
> ```

Spec 2.2 restates this as a constraint on every later artifact: the player's
biggest growth moments are also their most dangerous moments. Balance (A-007)
tunes the tradeoff; it may not delete it. No upgrade may make large mass free
of collision risk or stability cost. Upgrades may shift the dial between
"grow fast" and "stay precise" but may not switch the tension off (spec 13).

### 5.5 Starting black hole types (qualitative stats to be given numbers here)

**Star Mass Black Hole** — stable, reliable, slow-growth: stable mass growth,
reliable pull, better absorption efficiency, lower volatility, good for
beginners. Stats: Mass stable / Pull medium / Efficiency high / Stability high /
Speed medium.

**Quantum Black Hole** — unstable, high-power, high-risk: stronger pull, faster
growth, more volatile, emits quantum particles, phase-shift absorption modes,
chain-reacts with anomalies, higher risk of instability. Stats: Mass volatile /
Pull high / Efficiency medium / Stability low / Speed high.

### 5.6 Physics model (shape fixed by spec; constants are A-007's)

```text
massGain = bodyMass * efficiency
eventRadius = baseRadius + a * sqrt(mass)
pullStrength = pullScale * mass^0.75
```

Attraction:

```text
direction = blackHole.pos - body.pos
distanceSquared = direction.lengthSquared() + epsilon
force = pullStrength * bodyMass / distanceSquared
body.velocity += direction.normalized() * force * deltaTime
```

Absorption:

```text
if distance(body, blackHole) < absorptionRadius:
    absorb(body)
```

Arcade physics, not full real astrophysics.

```text
mass gain is direct

```

### 5.11 Failure conditions

Possible: time limit; stability reaches zero; too many hazard collisions; mass
drops below threshold. Recommended: `Time limit + stability + hazard pressure`.

### 5.13 Endgame modes

Campaign, endless mode, daily challenge, weekly challenge, leaderboards.

### 5.14 Monetization constraints on economy

Free-to-play; **no pay-to-win**; optional ads; optional IAP for cosmetics;
optional ad removal. Permanent upgrades must be reachable through play; no
upgrade may require purchase. Monetization must not block core game completion.

## Upstream excerpts — A-004 docs/spec.md

### Spec 3.2 loop nesting (durations confirmed by A-006)

| Layer | Duration | What the player is doing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Micro loop | 1–5 s | Attract and absorb one body or cluster |
| Meso loop | 20–60 s | Chain absorptions, build combo, clear a region |
| Macro loop | one level | Reach the level goal before failure |
| Meta loop | one session | Finish levels, spend permanent upgrades |

The combo window length is owned by A-007.

### Spec 6.1–6.4 objectives

- 6.1: Reach the level's target mass before the time limit expires. Pairing
  target-mass + timer as the *default* primary goal is a proposal flagged for
  A-007 to decide (spec 17 question 10).
- 6.2: A level may combine one primary goal with at most one secondary goal;
  secondary grants an extra reward, never replaces primary. Which levels use
  which goals and what rewards are is A-009's; the **rewards economy is A-007's**.
- 6.3: Run wins when primary goal satisfied before any failure fires; run ends
  immediately on win (result screen shows surplus).
- 6.4: Run loses the moment any one failure condition fires. No partial-credit
  system; failure conditions are cumulative resources (stability drains), so
  pressure accumulates without sudden single-hit losses. Hazard damage values
  owned by A-007/A-008.

### Spec 7.1–7.4 failure conditions

- Canonical set: `Time limit + stability + hazard pressure`. "Mass drops below
  threshold" is NOT canonical; may appear as a modifier on specific challenge
  levels (A-009's).
- Time limit: target mass not reached before timer expires → player takes
  riskier routes or invests in pull/efficiency upgrades.
- Stability: hazard contact and **oversized absorptions** drain stability;
  player decides whether to keep growing or hold a smaller, safer profile.
- Hazard pressure: hazard collisions drain stability (and/or mass; exact damage
  owned by A-007/A-008).
- Failure creates decisions, not punishment: every failure condition is visible
  on the HUD as a resource with headroom; stability is the decision resource
  (growing bigger/faster spends stability pressure; playing precise conserves
  it — a risk dial, not luck); no failure may be caused by an unavoidable
  hazard spawn; retry is immediate and free (no lives, no currency, no ad/IAP
  gate).

### Spec 8.1–8.3 player experience

- Feel: small and curious → in control → increasingly large → under pressure
  (bigger = slower, riskier, less precise) → rewarded for chaining and route
  choices, not mashing.
- Tension peaks at the moment of largest growth: the biggest absorb is also the
  most dangerous maneuver. A-006 (feedback) and A-007 (tuning) must both serve
  it.
- One decision at a time on screen: field, horizon ring, goal meter are the
  only always-read elements.

### Spec 9.2 growth model shape

Same formulas as PLAN 5.6 above. The spec fixes the **shape**; the constants
`a`, `pullScale`, `epsilon`, base radii, and every threshold belong to A-007.

### Spec 11.1 within a run

Grows: mass, event horizon radius, pull strength, absorption radius, combo
streak, temporary run-upgrade stat modifiers. Shrinks: movement speed (PLAN
5.10), stability under pressure. Temporary run upgrade reference values are in
PLAN 5.9 above. Spec rule: choices must be meaningful, not strictly additive —
a player must be able to build pull-heavy or efficiency-heavy; active temporary
upgrades must be visible on the HUD while active.

### Spec 11.2 across the campaign

Permanent meta-progression uses the PLAN 5.9 named set. Spec does not assign
effects; **effects and costs are A-007's** (definitions of what the items are
is A-008's). Permanent upgrades must be reachable through play; no purchase
required. Reference target masses quoted above. Campaign: five worlds (Dust
Belt, Asteroid Field, Ship Graveyard, Planet Ring, Quantum Nebula), each
teaching one escalation; world layout, level count (30–60), unlock system, and
difficulty curve are A-009's.

### Spec 13 upgrade systems

Both systems required. Tension rule applied to upgrades: an upgrade may shift
the dial between "grow fast" and "stay precise" but may not switch off the
tension of spec 2.2.

## Upstream excerpts — A-006 docs/design.md

- **Camera ring guarantee (design §2):** the zoom function must keep the full
  event horizon ring on screen with viewport-scaled margin; the exact zoom
  function is shared A-005/A-006/A-007, and A-007 tunes it under this
  constraint. Punch-in only on large absorbs.
- **Feedback ladder (design §6.2):** escalation is continuous with mass in
  three bands — low mass (thin calm ring, quiet), mid mass (ring brightens,
  pressure rises), high mass (ring pulses faster, field darkens, movement reads
  heavy). A-007 defines the mass thresholds where bands change; feedback must
  not imply a specific combo streak length.
- **Pacing (design §8, confirmed):** micro loop 1–5 s, meso loop 20–60 s are
  feel targets A-007's pull strengths, absorb radii, timers, and combo window
  must land inside. Combo window length is A-007's.
- **Stability anchor (design §6.1):** stability decays are communicated by ring
  fracture + pulse rhythm; stability meter must use segmented geometry and tick
  rhythm, not color alone. A-007 defines the stability numbers behind that
  language.
