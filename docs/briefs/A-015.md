## 3. Game identity for the listing (from approved A-004 spec, summary)

- Title: Event Horizon. Platform: iOS, Android.
- Elevator: the player controls a small black hole moving through space; it attracts nearby bodies with a gravitational field; bodies that reach it are absorbed; mass grows, reach grows, but speed and control pay costs; survive hazards, chase stars, chase the horizon.
- Modes to name in the copy: 40-level campaign in five worlds, endless, daily/weekly (A-009). Accessibility-forward (A-012: one-handed tilt default, reduced motion, high contrast, colorblind-safe palettes) — worth one copy line.
- No violence, no horror, no user-generated content, no in-game gambling. Optional cosmetic IAP and optional ads are disclosed by A-016/A-017; the age rating here must be consistent with that disclosure (flag the dependency as a compliance hook).
