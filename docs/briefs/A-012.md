## 2. The design rule every option must survive (PLAN §8)

> Do not make the game a pure power fantasy. More mass should create tension:
> stronger pull, larger collision risk, slower precise movement, harder
> navigation, stability may decay. Real decisions: grow fast and risk
> instability? stay smaller and move precisely?

Accessibility consequence: **options change how information is carried and how
intent is expressed — never what the decisions are.** No option may soften the
cost of mass, auto-aim, auto-absorb, or hide a lever. State this rule
explicitly and check every option against it.

## 3. Anchors from A-006 docs/design.md (approved) — preserve, never renegotiate

A-006 §7 "Accessibility direction" (A-006 sets anchors; A-012 owns
implementations):

- **Multi-channel redundancy:** every critical state (stability, hazard, combo,
  goal gap, failure type) is carried by at least two channels: shape, motion,
  rhythm, audio. **Color is never the sole carrier of any state.**
- **Stability anchor:** ring fracture (smooth → broken → dashed → flickering)
  + pulse rhythm + audio creak (§6.1). A-012's reduced-motion, high-contrast,
  and mute variants must preserve legibility of **all** stability states.
  Reduced-motion variant replaces animated flicker with **static dash density
  (more breaks = less stability)**; high-contrast variant **thickens the ring
  stroke**.
- **Hazard language is shape-first:** spiky silhouette + marker glyph;
  colorblind-safe palettes must **keep the glyph, not just swap hues**.
- **Ring contrast:** ring must pass contrast against every world background;
  concrete values are A-010/A-012's to set.
- **Minimum sizes:** ring stroke ≥ 2 px screen-space; edge indicators ≥ 8 px
  on the smallest supported phone.
- **Reduced motion:** all feedback (punch, shake, ripple, flicker) must have
  static-equivalent variants that keep information — you define them.
- **Text:** tutorial teaches by play, not text walls; text aids are additive.

Hard requirement (A-006): **every stability state must stay legible with color
disabled, motion frozen, and audio muted simultaneously.**

## 4. The A-010 settings surface you implement (names fixed by A-010, you own behavior)

A-010 S7 options with owner A-012: **UI scale** (within A-010's band clamp §7.3),
**safe-area reflow**, **reduced motion** (freezes flicker/pulse/chip
transitions; static fallbacks in A-010 §4.2/§5 must remain legible), **high
contrast** (raises stroke weight and background separation; must not change
glyph shapes), **colorblind palette** (replaces palette only; shape-first
encoding stays primary), **larger controls** (grows touch targets to the 72
ru tier; reflows bottom band), **text size** (numerals stay tabular), **audio
cues on/off** (A-010 §4.2 must stay legible with audio off), **haptics**
(on/off, no layout effect). A-010 also lists testability hooks: stability
stage and pressure-cluster state distinguishable with colorblind palette on /
high contrast off; pips distinguishable by shape alone; flicker stage
distinguishable from dashed stage under reduced motion; stability decay legible
with audio off through segment count and tick spacing.

A-010 open questions you must answer:
- **Q3:** is the flicker-stage static fallback (4 segments + halved tick
  spacing) enough under reduced motion for a player who cannot parse arc
  geometry, or is a text stability readout needed beyond the percentage?
- **Q4:** high contrast raises stroke weight — confirm a 3 ru ring stroke still
  fits the ring guarantee's margin budget (A-010 §3.3 sizes the ring to the
  narrower axis with margin), or shrink ring diameter by 4 ru in that mode.

## 5. Hooks from A-011 docs/input.md (approved) — you implement them

- **Tilt calibration** (A-011 §4): neutral-pose capture, ±4° default Hold
  tolerance; you own accessibility variants (wider tolerance, one-handed grip
  profiles, re-calibration prompts).
- **One-handed modes:** tilt needs no screen contact; joystick keeps thumbs
  out of play area. A-011 open question: which alternative is offered by
  default to one-handed players — your call.
- **Larger-controls stick geometry:** the 72 ru tier fits A-011's ≥ 180 ru
  control zone without moving pause — define the geometry.
- **Pause confirm-state variant:** an option on the existing single-tap 64 ru
  button, not a new verb in the default scheme — decide whether it ships and
  for whom.
